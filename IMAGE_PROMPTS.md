## Cross-Section Consistency Notes

- **Palette:** Warm Paper, Pure White, Architectural Ink, Graphite, Hairline, Signal Red, Status Green — identical across all 8 frames
- **Typography:** Satoshi Bold for headlines, Satoshi Regular for body, JetBrains Mono for ALL data/metadata/labels/buttons
- **Border radius:** Zero throughout — every edge is architectural
- **Shadow:** Zero throughout — elevation is structural (background shift), not optical
- **Section headers:** `/// LABEL` + hairline rule extension in every section that has one
- **CTA style:** Mix of ghost outline (1, 4, 7), primary pill (2, 6), underlined inline (3, 5), and banner (8). Varies per section, always monospace.
- **Section size rhythm:** Airy (1, 8) → Medium-dense (2, 3, 6) → Information-dense (4, 5, 7) → Airy (8). Alternates breathing room with data density.
- **Image treatment:** No photography. This is a typographic + structural design system. Color fields, rules, grids, and type carry all visual weight.
- **Tonal voice:** Precise, credible, calm. "Backend engineer" energy — no marketing, no hype, just calibrated information delivery.
